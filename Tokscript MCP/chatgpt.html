How it works

How TokScript MCP Works With ChatGPT

Connected in 30 seconds. Useful immediately

STEP 1
Open ChatGPT
Go to chatgpt.com and start a new conversation, or open the ChatGPT desktop app.
STEP 2
Click the Tools Icon
In the message input bar, click the tools or plug icon and select "Add tools" or "Connect more tools."
STEP 3
Paste Connection Link
Select "Add a custom MCP" and paste the following URL: https://api.tokscript.com/mcp
STEP 4
Authorize & Connect
Follow the redirect to TokScript, sign in with Google or email, and click "Allow" to finish.

How to verify it works

Start a new conversation in ChatGPT and paste any TikTok, Instagram, or YouTube link. Ask ChatGPT to transcribe it. If you receive a transcript and engagement stats, your connection is active.
